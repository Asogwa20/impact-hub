# Impact Hub

Build a premium, modern, responsive institutional website for:

IMPACT YOUTH DEVELOPMENT INITIATIVE (IYDI

IYDI is a Nigerian, youth-focused development organization committed to empowering young people and women to become productive, innovative, responsible, and active contributors to sustainable community and national development.

IMPORTANT:

This is the official organizational website for IYDI, NOT just a website for one event.

The website must present IYDI as a credible, established development organization while also highlighting its flagship initiative, the Impact Business Innovation Summit.

Do not invent facts, statistics, event dates, speakers, awards, partnerships, social media accounts, or achievements that were not

 provided.

TECHNOLOGY & DEVELOPMENT APPROACH

Use a clean modern React-based architecture suitable for export and further editing in VS Code.

Preferred:

- React

- TypeScript where appropriate

- Tailwind CSS

- Reusable components

- Clean component structure

- Responsive design

- Semantic HTML

- Accessible navigation

- SEO-friendly page structure

- Fast loading

- Optimized images

- Easy-to-maintain content/data structures

Do NOT add:

- User authentication

- Payment system

- Shopping/e-commerce

- Complex backend

- Database requirements

- Fake dashboards

- Fake statistics

The site should work primarily as a professional content-driven website.

For events, create reusable event data structures/components so future events can easily be added or edited from the code.

Registration links should be designed so they can be added later.

Do NOT create a Google Form yet.

BRAND & VISUAL DIRECTION

Create a premium, trustworthy, professional African development-organization aesthetic.

The design should communicate:

- Youth empowerment

- Leadership

- Innovation

- Entrepreneurship

- Women empowerment

- Community development

- Education

- Technology

- Sustainable development

- Collaboration

- Opportunity

Visual style:

- Modern

- Professional

- Human-centered

- Institutional but youthful

- Elegant

- Inspiring

- High-end NGO/development organization quality

Avoid:

- Generic charity-template appearance

- Excessive gradients

- Overly playful children-oriented visuals

- Cheap-looking cards

- Excessive glassmorphism

- Excessive animations

- Cluttered layouts

Use a strong visual hierarchy, excellent typography, generous spacing, premium cards, subtle shadows, tasteful rounded corners, strong photography and clean sections.

Use the official IYDI logo supplied by the client.

Do not redesign or alter the logo.

Create a cohesive colour system around the official logo. If exact brand colours are not available, derive a professional colour palette from the logo rather than inventing an unrelated palette.

Use subtle animations:

- Fade-in

- Slide-up

- Hover transitions

- Smooth scrolling

- Image reveal effects

Animations must remain professional and performant.

3. WEBSITE STRUCTURE

Create these primary navigation items

HOME

ABOUT

PROGRAMMES

EVENTS

IMPACT

GALLERY

LEADERSHIP

CONTACT

Include a prominent CTA:

“Explore Our Programmes”

and/or

“View Upcoming Events

The navigation must be responsive with a high-quality mobile menu.

HOME PAGE

Create a strong hero section.

Hero messaging should communicate that IYDI empowers young people and women through skills, entrepreneurship, innovation, leadership, mentorship and opportunities.

Suggested headline direction:

“Empowering Young People and Women to Create Impact”

Supporting text should be concise and based only on the supplied organizational information.

Hero CTA buttons:

- Explore Our Programmes

- View Upcoming Events

Use authentic organizational imagery where available.

Immediately after the hero, create a concise introduction section explaining what IYDI does.

Add a “What We Do” / programme preview section showing the 9 programme areas.

Programme areas:

1. Agriculture and Agribusiness

2. Entrepreneurship and Business Development

3. Skills and Employability

4. Technology and Innovation

5. Fashion, Creativity and Creative Economy

6. Leadership and Governance

7. Women’s Empowerment

8. Education and Mentorship

9. Peacebuilding and Community Development

Create a visually strong “How We Create Impact” section based on:

IDENTIFY → EMPOWER → CONNECT → SUPPORT → MEASURE

Create a flagship initiative section for:

IMPACT BUSINESS INNOVATION SUMMIT

Explain that it brings together:

- Young people

- Women

- Entrepreneurs

- Investors

- Business leaders

- Innovators

- Policymakers

- Development organizations

- Other stakeholders

Highlight activities such as:

- Entrepreneurship education

- Business development

- Innovation and technology exposure

- Leadership and capacity building

- Mentorship

- Networking

- Investment opportunities

- Business idea competitions

- Market/investor linkages

Add a “Who We Serve” section.

Target beneficiaries:

- Young people and emerging entrepreneurs

- Women and women-led businesses

- Students and recent graduates

- Early-stage entrepreneurs

- Small and growing businesses

- Innovators and technology enterprises

- Young professionals and creatives

- Vulnerable and underserved youth

- Community-based groups and organizations

Add a concise vision/mission section.

Add a gallery preview using event photographs.

Add a CTA section:

“Be Part of the Impact”

Encourage visitors to:

- Explore programmes

- Attend events

- Partner with IYDI

- Connect with the organization

Add a professional footer.
