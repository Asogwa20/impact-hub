Our impact

Potential into
meaningful progress.

IYDI’s approach connects empowerment with opportunities to contribute to sustainable community and national development.

The IYDI approach

Five stages.
One purpose.

A development framework for identifying needs and opportunities, supporting people, and learning from progress.

  1. 01

    Identify

    Identify challenges, talents, ideas and opportunities.

  2. 02

    Empower

    Provide knowledge, skills, training, mentorship and resources.

  3. 03

    Connect

    Connect beneficiaries with mentors, investors, institutions, markets, employers and development partners.

  4. 04

    Support

    Provide continued guidance, technical assistance and post-programme engagement.

  5. 05

    Measure

    Track outcomes, lessons and impact for continuous improvement.

Expected impact areas

The change we
work towards.

These are intended outcomes of IYDI’s development approach—not reported or achieved results.

  • Increased youth and women entrepreneurship
  • Improved employability and income generation
  • Increased access to skills and education
  • Growth of youth and women-led businesses
  • Increased mentorship and investment opportunities
  • Greater youth participation in community development
  • Increased innovation and technology adoption
  • Reduced youth vulnerability to harmful behaviours
  • Stronger entrepreneur, investor, institution and market networks
  • More responsible and ethical youth leaders
  • Stronger and more resilient communities

Our vision

An inclusive and
prosperous society.

To build an inclusive and prosperous society where young people and women are empowered, economically independent, innovative, responsible, and actively contributing to sustainable development.

Our mission

Empowerment for
sustainable livelihoods.

To empower young people and women through capacity development, entrepreneurship, innovation, education, mentorship, advocacy, partnerships, and access to opportunities, enabling them to build sustainable livelihoods and become active contributors to social and economic development.

Learning & accountability

Impact beyond numbers.

IYDI’s focus is on the contribution young people and women can make as productive, innovative, responsible and active members of their communities.

Verified programme outcomes and impact reports will be published when available. No achieved impact figures are currently published here.
