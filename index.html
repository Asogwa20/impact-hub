Who we are

Potential is everywhere.
Opportunity should be, too.

Impact Youth Development Initiative (IYDI) is a Nigerian, youth-focused development organization committed to empowering young people and women.

We connect skills, entrepreneurship, innovation, leadership, mentorship and opportunities with sustainable community and national development.

Who we serve

Ambition has
many faces.

Our focus is on people and enterprises with the potential to shape stronger, more inclusive communities.

  • Young people and emerging entrepreneurs
  • Women and women-led businesses
  • Students and recent graduates
  • Early-stage entrepreneurs
  • Small and growing businesses
  • Innovators and technology enterprises
  • Young professionals and creatives
  • Vulnerable and underserved youth
  • Community-based groups and organizations

Our vision

A future shaped by
empowered people.

To build an inclusive and prosperous society where young people and women are empowered, economically independent, innovative, responsible, and actively contributing to sustainable development.

Our mission

Turning potential
into participation.

To empower young people and women through capacity development, entrepreneurship, innovation, education, mentorship, advocacy, partnerships, and access to opportunities, enabling them to build sustainable livelihoods and become active contributors to social and economic development.
